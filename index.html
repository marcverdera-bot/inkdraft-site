How it works

Five steps. You decide at every one.

  1. I
    Step 1 · Brief

    Describe the request

    Write it the way your client said it: subject, placement and size. No brief? Hit Surprise me and explore.

  2. II
    Step 2 · Ideas

    Pick the idea

    Four ideas with a twist, in words. Exploring ideas is free: ask for four more as often as you like.

  3. III
    Step 3 · Concepts

    Pick the concept

    Four drawings of the chosen idea, in your style. None of them right? Ask for four more.

  4. IV
    Step 4 · Stencil

    Take the stencil

    Clean, single-weight linework, true to the concept. Shading and style are yours to add in Procreate.

  5. V
    Step 5 · On skin

    Show your client

    Place the design on skin, adjust the size and send the preview before the appointment.

FAQ

What people ask

What is Inkdraft?

Inkdraft is an AI tattoo design tool for tattoo artists. It turns your client's brief into four ideas, four drawn concepts and a single-weight stencil ready for Procreate, with an on-skin preview.

Which tattoo styles does it support?

Blackwork, with antique engraving linework, and fineline, for small and delicate pieces. In beta, Your style: Inkdraft suggests ideas based on 5 to 10 of your own designs.

How much does it cost?

Free trial with 3 designs, no card. Starter, €19 a month (10 designs); Pro, €39 a month (30 designs); Studio, €99 a month (100 designs, up to 5 artists). The 50 founder seats get the Pro plan at €19 a month for life.

Does it work with Procreate?

Yes. The stencil exports ready to open in Procreate and as a PNG, so you can add shading and style your way.

Do I own the designs?

Yes. What you generate is yours to tattoo, post and sell.

Is it AI?

Yes, for generating ideas and concepts. The design judgement (composition, anatomy, what works on skin) comes from us. The finish comes from you.

Can I tattoo straight from it?

The stencil is ready for Procreate. We always recommend your own final pass: size, placement and style are your call.

When can I start using it?

Founders get into the beta in early December 2026. Until then, we send you hand-drawn designs for your real client requests.

Can I cancel?

Any time. If you cancel a founder seat, you lose the lifetime price.
