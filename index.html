How it works

Five steps. You decide at every one.

A real brief, made with Inkdraft from start to finish. Every image comes straight out of the app, untouched.

  1. I
    Step 1 · Brief

    Describe the request

    Write it the way your client said it: subject, placement and size. If they sent reference photos, add them: Inkdraft reads them as taste and never copies them.

    “beetle fused with a rose”

    Placement
    Forearm
    Size
    15 cm
    Style
    Blackwork
    Typed in a hurry, just like a real brief.
  2. II
    Step 2 · Concepts

    Pick by eye

    Four different concepts, each one drawn. You choose by looking, not by reading descriptions. None of them right? Ask for another round.

  3. III
    Step 3 · Variations

    Refine it

    Three variations of the one you picked: close, medium and bold. To fix a detail or try another angle.

  4. IV
    Step 4 · Stencil

    Take the stencil

    Clean, single-weight linework, true to the drawing, at 2048 px. Open it in Procreate and add the shading your way.

  5. V
    Step 5 · On skin

    Show your client

    Upload a photo of the placement, position the design with your fingers and Inkdraft makes it look real. Your client sees it before the appointment.

FAQ

What people ask

What is Inkdraft?

Inkdraft is an AI tattoo design tool for tattoo artists. It turns your client's brief into four drawn concepts, variations of the one you pick and a single-weight stencil ready for Procreate, with an on-skin preview.

Which tattoo styles does it support?

Today, blackwork with antique engraving linework. In progress: fineline and traditional. Want another one? Tell us when you claim your founder seat.

How much does it cost?

Free trial with 3 designs, no card. Starter, €19 a month (10 designs); Pro, €39 a month (30 designs); Studio, €99 a month (100 designs, up to 5 artists). The 50 founder seats get the Pro plan at €19 a month for life.

Does it work with Procreate?

Yes. The stencil exports ready to open in Procreate and as a PNG, so you can add shading and style your way.

Do I own the designs?

Yes. What you generate is yours to tattoo, post and sell.

Is it AI?

Yes, for generating ideas and concepts. The design judgement (composition, anatomy, what works on skin) comes from us. The finish comes from you.

Can I tattoo straight from it?

The stencil is ready for Procreate. We always recommend your own final pass: size, placement and style are your call.

When can I start using it?

Founders get into the beta in early December 2026. Until then, we send you hand-drawn designs for your real client requests.

Can I cancel?

Any time. If you cancel a founder seat, you lose the lifetime price.
